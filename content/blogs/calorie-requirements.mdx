Understanding your calorie requirements is fundamental to achieving your
health and fitness goals. Here's a practical guide to calculating and
applying this knowledge.

## The Basics

Your **Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE)** is the number of calories you
burn in a day. It consists of several components:

- **Basal Metabolic Rate (BMR):** Calories burned at rest
- **Activity Level:** Calories from daily movement
- **Exercise:** Structured physical activity
- **Thermogenesis:** Calories burned digesting food
